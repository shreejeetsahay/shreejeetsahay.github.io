Research

I am interested in explainability and interpretability of Large Language Models (LLMs). I am still exploring the field, and I am open to collaborations and discussions.

Attributed Graph Alignment for Circuit Distillation
Master's Thesis — In Progress, advised by Dr. Jundong Li, and guided by Zhenyu Lei

My thesis reformulates head mapping in mechanistic knowledge distillation of LLMs as attributed graph alignment, replacing the ablation heuristic, and reduces mapping degeneracy by replacing greedy matching with transport-based alignment.

Assessing the Efficacy of Attention Rollout for Post-hoc Interpretability in Vision Transformers
Shreejeet Sahay, Devaaya Latta, Sabrina Lopez, Michael Jerge, Arman Hosseini, Rishal Jagtap
Course Project — UVA CS 6501 Neural Networks

We evaluated the efficacy of the attention rollout mechanism for post-hoc interpretability in Vision Transformers by performing single-object, multi-object, and embedding analyses on DeiT-Small, ViT-Base, and DETR models.

Paper / Code
Vehicular CO2 Emission Prediction Using LSTM Network
Shreejeet Sahay, Pranav Pawar, Yogita Wagh*, Dipesh Sonawane**
Springer Geography (IMS 2023) / Extended version in the International Journal of Open Information Technologies (INJOIT)

We proposed a feasible, efficient, and scalable system composed of a 2-layer LSTM network to predict vehicular CO2 emissions in real-time on On-Board Diagnostics-II (OBD-II) data.
